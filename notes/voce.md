# Textul de citit — vocea videoului (varianta 6)

**Cum înregistrezi:** telefonul la ~20 cm, cameră liniștită, **un fișier pe paragraf** (`s01.m4a`, `s02.m4a`…),
1 s de liniște la început și la sfârșit, citit rar și calm. Dacă greșești, reiei doar paragraful.
Pronunție: lei = „lay” · 8,307 = „eight thousand three hundred and seven” · 8,500 = „eight thousand five hundred” · ANAF = „A-N-A-F” sau „ah-naf”.

**s01 (~13 s)**
This payslip says 8,500 lei net. Romanian payroll rules say 8,307. Today a credit analyst catches that by hand: read, recalculate, cross-check. About twenty minutes a file, by our own timing.

**s02 (~15 s)**
We built ORB to help credit analysts check loan applications. Salary data from ANAF can't cover everything, so ORB checks the documents themselves against the bank's rules, and flags exactly what's wrong.

**s03 (~10 s)**
Watch the names. Maria's application: three synthetic documents. Before any AI sees them, the Guard swaps every name and ID number for a label.

**s04 (~26 s)**
Six agents, at double speed. The Clerk extracts the fields; any value it can't point to in the document is discarded. Now the findings. The Verifier recomputes the tax: net should be 8,307, not 8,500. The employer, in our demo registry: inactive. The salary never arrives in her statement. One click, and the box lands on the exact spot.

**s05 (~7 s)**
The Detector marks the edited pixels. The Detective finds one bank account in six applications.

**s06 (~17 s)**
Watch the button. The risk comes from fixed rules, every finding listed. The AI only writes the summary, and a summary quoting a number not in the evidence is thrown out. The analyst decides, with a written reason.

**s07 (~18 s)**
Different file now: a blurry phone photo. ORB can't guarantee it hid everything, so its text never goes to the cloud AI; a person gets it. And a sum the OCR may have misread is re-read from the image: 'to check', not 'forged'.

**s08 (~16 s)**
Why this shape? Fixed steps, because an audit needs the same steps every time. The AI only reads and summarises; every decision is a written rule. If an agent fails, the rest finish, and the file is never shown as clean.

**s09 (~10 s)**
Most files are honest: clean in seconds, every value linked to its source. Twenty minutes becomes about a minute of review, our estimate.

**s10 (~12 s)**
Built for a bank: the cloud AI sees only labels, and a second gate blocks any request still carrying a name. One switch runs it all in-house.

**s11 (~18 s)**
Sixty synthetic files, real cloud AI: no name or ID number left the bank. None of twelve honest applicants flagged high-risk; three went to a human. All thirty-six simple forgeries caught, and all twelve perfect ones sent to a human. The forgeries are ours.

**s12 (~10 s)**
Six modules, one contract, 275 tests, one command. ORB checks documents, not creditworthiness. Our ask: a pilot on a hundred real files, in-house.

**s13 (~4 s)**
ORB does the checking. Your people decide.
